About Me

Web Developer | Graphic Designer | IT Ausbildung Applicant

I’m a motivated web developer from Pakistan with around 2–3 years of practical, hands-on experience building real websites for real clients. I’m now preparing for the next step: an IT Ausbildung in Germany, where I can turn my existing skills into a strong professional foundation.

My experience includes responsive website development, landing pages, e-commerce stores, UI/UX and graphic design, on-page SEO, and AI-assisted development workflows. I enjoy creating websites that are not only functional, but also clean, modern and visually engaging.

Alongside development, I work as a graphic designer and use Figma for UI design and prototyping. I’m comfortable combining design and development to build complete digital experiences rather than focusing on only one part of a project.

For me, an Ausbildung is the right next step because I want to learn in a professional German work environment, gain real industry experience, strengthen my technical and language skills, and grow into a reliable, well-rounded IT professional.
